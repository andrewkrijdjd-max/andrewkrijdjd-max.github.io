Common questions from buyers

Clear answers on ownership, hosting, customization, and payments—no fine print.

Are there really zero monthly license fees?

Yes. Every Kreta Software package is sold as a one-time delivery. You receive the complete source code, SQL schema, Docker files, and spreadsheet assets under your ownership—no per-seat renewals or vendor lock-in.

Where does the software run? Do you charge for hosting?

No—we never resell or lock you into proprietary hosting. Each platform ships with a 1-command Docker Compose setup so you can run it on your own server, cloud account (AWS, GCP, Render, VPS), or local network, paying your provider directly.

Can we add our logo, fields, or custom reports?

Yes. Branding and configuration variables are built in, and any extra workflows or custom integrations are quoted transparently up front as a fixed-scope project.

How do international payments and milestones work?

Projects are billed in milestones (50/50 or 40/30/30). We accept Payoneer (USD ACH), platform Escrow (Upwork/Freelancer), and Binance Pay / USDT (which includes an automatic 5% prompt-payment discount).

What happens if we need changes or support after delivery?

Every system includes a corrective warranty period after delivery. Optional post-delivery support is strictly on-demand (single work order or prepaid 5-hour pack)—never an automatic recurring subscription.
