Questions buyers ask most

Straightforward answers on ownership, setup, team scaling, and payments.

Are there really zero monthly fees after I buy?

Yes. You pay once and receive the complete source code, database, and spreadsheet templates under your company's ownership. No recurring seat licenses ever.

Does the price change if my team grows?

Initial implementation is sized by organization tier (up to 15 users, 16–35 users, or 36+ users) so setup and onboarding match your complexity. Once delivered, you own the software with zero per-user monthly charges.

Where is our data stored? Do you control our hosting?

Your data stays 100% under your control. Every system ships ready to run in 1 command on your own server, cloud account (AWS, Google Cloud, Render, VPS), or local office computer—you never depend on us for hosting.

How do payments work internationally?

We work in safe milestones (50/50 or 40/30/30). You can pay via Payoneer (USD ACH), freelance Escrow (Upwork/Freelancer), or Binance Pay / USDT (which includes an instant 5% prompt-payment discount).

Can we start with Google Sheets and upgrade later?

Yes. You can launch with the Google Sheets Edition today and upgrade to the full Web Portal + SQL Edition whenever you are ready.
