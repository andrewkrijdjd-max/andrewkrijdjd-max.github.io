            FAQ
            
Technical & Procurement Questions

          

        

        

          

            Is this genuinely a one-time payment with zero recurring license fees?
            
Yes. Every edition is delivered as a closed fixed-scope project under a single one-time fee (50% kick-off, 50% upon live acceptance). There are no mandatory monthly subscriptions, per-seat fees, or auto-renewals.

          

          

            How do HIPAA, GDPR, or internal IT security reviews work with self-hosting?
            
Because you deploy the SQL + Web Portal on your own local server, VPC, or office machine via Docker Compose, Kreta Software never stores, touches, or has access to your production data. You control encryption at rest, backups, firewall rules, and user access.

          

          

            Can we import our existing spreadsheets and start with Google Sheets first?
            
Yes. Every system supports clean CSV import/export out of the box. And if you start with the Google Sheets edition, 100% of your initial payment is credited if you upgrade to SQL + Web Portal or Hybrid later.

          

          

            What warranty and post-delivery support are included?
            
Every delivery includes a 90-day corrective defect warranty within the agreed scope at zero cost. Future feature additions are handled strictly on demand via fixed work orders or a prepaid 5-hour pack valid for 12 months—never forced retainers.

          

          

            Which international payment and escrow options are available?
            
US ACH (USD) and EU SEPA (EUR) bank transfers, milestone escrow via Upwork/Escrow.com, local bank transfer with invoice in Argentina, or USDT / Binance Pay (with a 5% early-payment discount).
