Frequently Asked Questions

Is this genuinely a one-time payment with no recurring license fees?

Yes. Every edition is delivered as a closed fixed-scope project under a single one-time fee (typically split 50/50 across milestones). There are no mandatory monthly subscriptions, per-user seat fees, or automatic renewals.

Where does the software run and who owns the database?

You retain 100% ownership of the source code and database. It runs locally, on your own server via Docker, or on any cloud host you contract directly—never locked inside a third-party SaaS vendor.

Can we start with the Google Sheets edition and upgrade later?

Yes. Any Google Sheets edition can be upgraded to SQL + Web Portal or Hybrid later with 100% of your initial payment credited toward the upgrade.

What support or maintenance is available after delivery?

Each delivery includes a corrective warranty for defects within the agreed scope at zero cost. Optional future changes or maintenance are handled strictly on demand via fixed work orders or a prepaid 5-hour pack valid for 12 months—never recurring retainers.

Which payment methods are accepted internationally?

US/EU B2B bank transfer (ACH USD / SEPA EUR via Payoneer/DolarApp), escrow via Upwork/Fiverr, local bank transfer with invoice in Argentina, or USDT / Binance Pay (with a 5% early-payment discount).
